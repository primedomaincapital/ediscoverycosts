The opportunity

A $42 billion line item with no price tape.

When a company is sued, the evidence lives in inboxes, chat logs, and shared drives — terabytes of it. Collecting, processing, hosting, and reviewing that data is eDiscovery, and it consumes roughly a fifth of all American litigation spend. Yet per-gigabyte processing fees run from $29 to $135 with no published benchmark, and general counsels approve invoices they cannot decode.

EDiscoveryCosts.com names the missing dataset. It is the natural home for the industry's first eDiscovery cost index — processing rates by matter size, review costs per document, hosting benchmarks by vendor, and the true-cost multipliers every legal operations team wants and no one publishes.
